Car logo source set: [VehicleSpecs brand-logos](https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos) pinned to commit `3f0929e70e0a9a2a502063edc4b3e5c0146cba74`. Four additional files are individually sourced from Wikimedia Commons as shown above.

The following six compact SVG wordmark marks were drawn locally for makes that
were not present in the bundled source set: Aion, Dodge, Foton, Lifan, MG, and
Neta. They are lightweight picker artwork, not downloaded official logo files.

The repository README says its logo collection was curated and visually checked. It also notes that the marks remain the property of their brand owners. The Commons pages identify Li Auto and Exeed as simple text/geometric marks, Range Rover as CC BY-SA 4.0, and GAC as CC BY-SA 4.0 with permission recorded by Wikimedia's Volunteer Response Team.
